February 2025

QeMFi Dataset Published in Scientific Data

Our new dataset paper, QeMFi: A Multifidelity Dataset of Quantum Chemical Properties of Diverse Molecules, has been officially published in Scientific Data (Nature Portfolio). This work provides a comprehensive, structured dataset aimed at bridging the fidelity gap in sparse data regimes, allowing for robust benchmarking of multifidelity machine learning models in quantum chemistry.

January 2025

New Paper in The Journal of Chemical Physics

Thrilled to share that our research, Benchmarking data efficiency in Δ-ML and multifidelity models for quantum chemistry, is now out. We conduct extensive benchmarks to highlight how incorporating varying degrees of accuracy can drastically reduce computational costs without sacrificing predictive power.

Excitation Energy Transfer Study Published

I co-authored a collaborative study on Excitation Energy Transfer between Porphyrin Dyes on a Clay Surface. This research heavily employs Multifidelity Machine Learning techniques and was published in Advanced Theory and Simulations.
