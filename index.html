Vivin Vinod

Postdoctoral Researcher in Machine Learning & Computational Chemistry
University of Wuppertal, Germany

I am a postdoctoral researcher specializing in low-cost, high-accuracy multifidelity machine learning. My focus areas include quantum chemistry applications, active learning sampling strategies, and uncertainty quantification. With a multi-disciplinary academic background spanning physics, economics, computer science, and quantum chemistry, I aim to bridge the fidelity gap in sparse data regimes and explore interdisciplinary workflows.
